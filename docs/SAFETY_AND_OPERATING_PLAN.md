# Safety and operating plan

- Test with wheels lifted before floor tests.
- Keep a physical power cutoff within reach.
- Start with low throttle and a clear, small test area.
- The Uno watchdog is a last-resort stop, not a substitute for an emergency stop.
- Voice commands are intentionally limited to stop/pause/resume and named goals;
  ambiguous speech must produce no motion.
- Do not run autonomous navigation until lidar coverage, localization, and
  obstacle stopping have been tested independently.
